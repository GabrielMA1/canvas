Delivery standards

What every RielArt engagement is built to include.

Standards are established before production so the work is easier to review, use, own, and improve.

01

Clear scope and ownership

The goal, deliverables, responsibilities, dependencies, and approval points are documented before production moves forward.

02

Responsive and accessible foundations

Layouts, interactions, forms, and content are designed to work across devices and with practical accessibility in mind.

03

Performance and search readiness

Page structure, media, metadata, internal links, and measurement are considered from the beginning—not added as an afterthought.

04

Measurable paths

Important actions are defined so the business can understand what visitors do and where the experience needs improvement.

05

Maintainable handoff

Access, files, documentation, ownership, and next steps are treated as part of the deliverable.

06

Human judgment around AI

Automation handles repeatable work. Sensitive decisions, approvals, and exceptions retain a clear route to a person.

A visible delivery process

Know what is happening, what is needed, and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Review the business goal, audience, current experience, tools, constraints, and source material.

  2. 02

    Define

    Turn the findings into a prioritized scope, page or workflow plan, milestones, and success measures.

  3. 03

    Build

    Create the focused solution, review it in context, and keep decisions connected to the original goal.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Test responsive behavior, content, forms, accessibility, performance, integrations, and edge cases.

  5. 05

    Launch & Improve

    Deploy, document access, explain ownership, and identify the next useful phase.
