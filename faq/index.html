Services and fit

Process and timing

Planning the work

Timing depends on scope, content readiness, feedback, integrations, and third-party dependencies. Every engagement receives a documented sequence and milestone plan before production begins. See the five-phase process.

The starting checklist depends on the scope. It commonly includes the business goal, decision owners, existing brand or website material, relevant content, required access, known dependencies, and any evidence that explains the current problem. Missing items and responsibilities are identified during assessment.

Pricing and payment

Starting points and scoped work

Yes. The Pricing page includes focused starting points and monthly support. Larger or connected engagements are scoped around the actual work.

Prices shown on the website are in US dollars. Fixed-scope online orders use the payment links shown on the Pricing page, while custom work is governed by an accepted proposal or agreement. Content, ownership, and platforms

Maintaining control after delivery

Content responsibilities and ownership are documented in the project scope or agreement. RielArt can support content structure and copy guidance when included. Ownership and licensing for final work, pre-existing material, software, fonts, stock assets, and third-party components remain subject to the applicable agreement and original licence terms.

Platform selection is based on content, integrations, ownership, budget, and maintenance needs. Existing systems are reviewed before replacement is recommended, and the simplest maintainable option that meets the business need is preferred.

AI, data, and privacy

Use practical automation with clear boundaries

No. AI is used only where it improves a real process. A simpler rule, form, integration, or manual approval step is often the better solution.

The scope identifies approved data sources, access needs, workflow owners, exceptions, and where human review is required. Privacy, security, retention, and third-party terms must be considered for the actual tools and information involved. The website Privacy Policy and Terms explain how this website handles information and the general conditions that apply.
