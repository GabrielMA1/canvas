Services and starting point

The $599 USD one-time service includes a structured questionnaire, brand and positioning direction, a practical visual identity, up to four core website pages, a contact form, basic on-page SEO, mobile optimization, analytics and tracking foundations, domain connection and launch assistance, one organized revision round, Client Portal access, and final handoff.

Platform and advertising spend

No. The $349 USD monthly fee covers RielArt's management service. The client pays advertising spend directly to Google or Meta. Budgets generally begin around $500 USD per month, but the appropriate range depends on the industry, market, competition, offer, platform, and goals.
