05 · What is included

The written scope defines the complete deliverable.

Included work is limited to the selected package or plan, its listed allowance, and the accepted proposal or service agreement.

Defined work

Listed deliverables, the agreed review path, and the handoff described for the selected option.

Visible ownership

Required client inputs, RielArt responsibilities, approval points, and dependencies are confirmed before production.

Practical handoff

Relevant access, documentation, and next steps are treated as part of delivery.

06 · What is excluded

Costs and work outside the written scope remain separate.

Third-party software, premium themes or plugins, hosting, domains, messaging usage, complex content creation, premium assets, media spend, and work outside the written scope are priced separately unless explicitly included.
