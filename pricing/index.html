From $129 USD one time

Ask About Business Email

What’s included

  • One existing business domain
  • Up to 3 users for a straightforward new setup
  • Mailboxes and accounts created
  • Email connected to your domain (MX records)
  • Settings that help keep your email out of spam (SPF and DKIM)
  • Appropriate initial DMARC configuration, which helps stop fake emails from your domain
  • Two-step login and basic security
  • Shared addresses like info@ where useful
  • Client-owned administrative handoff: the account is yours

Provider costs are separate. Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 subscriptions are paid directly by you. Existing mailbox migration, larger teams, and multiple domains are quoted separately.
