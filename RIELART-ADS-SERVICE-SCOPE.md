# Focused Ads Management Scope

## Commercial terms

**RielArt management fee:** $349 USD per month  
**Initial commitment:** Three months  
**Advertising spend:** Paid separately by the client directly to Google or Meta  
**Standard platform:** One — Google Ads or Meta Ads  
**Standard market:** One country or one clearly defined regional market  
**Standard language and offer:** One advertising language and one primary service or offer

The initial period allows time to configure tracking, build and launch the campaign, gather useful data, and make informed improvements. It does not guarantee that meaningful results will occur within three months.

## Shared monthly scope

- Advertising-readiness review
- Platform recommendation
- One advertising platform
- One business and one website
- One country or defined regional market
- One advertising language
- One primary service or offer
- Campaign strategy and initial campaign setup
- Basic conversion tracking
- Advertising copy
- Monitoring and data-based optimization
- Monthly performance summary
- One monthly review call of up to 30 minutes
- Up to one hour of advertising-related website or landing-page changes
- Client Portal access

RielArt recommends Google or Meta after reviewing the business, offer, customer search behaviour, geographic market, available budget, website readiness, conversion path, visual assets, competition, and average customer value.

## Google Search Ads scope

When Google Search Ads is selected, the standard service includes:

- One Google Ads account
- One Search campaign
- Up to three focused ad groups
- One responsive search ad per ad group
- Multiple unique headlines and descriptions
- Keyword research and match-type review
- Search-term review and negative-keyword management
- Geographic targeting
- Schedule and device review
- Relevant sitelinks and callout assets
- Call assets where appropriate
- Website-form conversion tracking where technically possible
- Phone-call conversion tracking where technically possible
- Regular monitoring, monthly optimization, and monthly reporting

Google responsive search ads use multiple headlines and descriptions that may be combined and tested in different ways. RielArt manages campaign structure, targeting, messaging, tracking, and optimization rather than selling a fixed number of repeated advertisements.

The standard scope does not automatically include Display, YouTube, Performance Max, Shopping, ecommerce feeds, multiple unrelated campaigns, multiple businesses, multiple countries or languages, separate location strategies, advanced offline conversion integrations, extensive landing-page development, or advanced CRM implementation.

## Meta Ads scope

When Meta Ads is selected, the standard service includes:

- One Meta advertising account
- Facebook and Instagram placements through Meta Ads Manager
- One Leads or Sales campaign
- Up to two ad sets when justified
- Approximately three to five initial ad variations
- Advertising copy and headline variations
- Basic static creative using approved client-provided assets
- Up to two refreshed static creative variations per month when useful
- Basic audience configuration
- Geographic targeting and placement review
- Meta Pixel setup where supported
- Website-event review and testing
- Conversions API review where supported by the client website platform and scope
- Regular monitoring, monthly optimization, and monthly reporting

Creative refreshes are produced when campaign needs and suitable source assets support them; two new assets are not promised every month.

The standard scope does not automatically include organic social posting, account or community management, comment or direct-message responses, influencer marketing, professional photography or video, regular custom video ads, complex motion design, large creative volumes, ecommerce catalogues, multiple countries or languages, multiple unrelated offers, or advanced funnel systems.

## Platform fit

### Google Search Ads

Best when potential customers are already searching for the service. Potential fits include home services, professional services, local or urgent services, B2B services, and other high-intent searches.

### Meta Ads

Best when the offer benefits from strong visuals, awareness, promotions, or reaching customers before they actively search. Potential fits include beauty, fitness, events, consumer services, visual transformations, promotions, and new or unfamiliar offers.

These are examples, not qualification promises. RielArt confirms the recommendation after reviewing the offer, market, website, and budget.

## Website readiness

RielArt reviews the existing website or landing page for:

- offer and location clarity;
- mobile usability and page speed;
- working forms and contact methods;
- a clear call to action and basic trust signals;
- privacy information;
- tracking capability;
- advertising-policy compatibility.

When important issues would limit campaign performance, RielArt may recommend correcting the website or completing Brand & Website Launch first. Not every advertising client is required to buy the $599 service.

## Included website-edit allowance

The service includes up to one hour per month of minor website or landing-page changes that directly support the active advertising campaign.

Possible included work:

- adjusting a headline, short section, call to action, or button;
- replacing a small image;
- correcting contact information;
- updating a promotion;
- improving a simple form;
- adding or correcting tracking;
- making a small mobile-layout correction;
- improving campaign-message consistency.

Not included:

- new pages or websites;
- full-page redesigns or large layout changes;
- ecommerce or booking-system development;
- advanced integrations;
- extensive copywriting;
- technical work unrelated to advertising;
- unlimited changes or revisions.

Unused website-update time does not roll over.

When RielArt did not build the website, campaign-related edits are subject to a review of the platform, access, code, plugins, theme, and technical condition. Work that cannot be completed safely within the allowance requires separate approval and scope.

## Budget and billing

Advertising budgets generally begin around $500 USD per month, but the appropriate budget depends on the industry, location, competition, offer, platform, and goals.

- The advertising budget is not included in the $349 RielArt fee.
- The client pays advertising spend directly to Google or Meta.
- $500 is a starting reference, not a guaranteed sufficient budget.
- Competitive industries may require more.
- RielArt confirms a recommended range after reviewing the business.
- No maximum supported budget is publicly promised.

## International limits

The standard service includes one country or one clearly defined regional market, one advertising language, and one primary offer.

Multi-country or multilingual campaigns require separate review because they may need different targeting, copy, budgets, landing pages, policies, currencies, time zones, and reporting. No public add-on price table is offered.

## Account ownership and access

- The client owns the advertising account, campaign history, and data.
- The client adds and controls the platform payment method.
- RielArt receives manager, partner, or task access required to manage the account.
- Personal passwords must not be shared.
- The client must retain RielArt’s required access while active management is provided.
- Access may be removed after termination and handoff.

RielArt should use Google Ads Manager Account access and Meta business partner or task access where applicable. An account that should belong to the client must not be created under RielArt’s ownership.

## Reporting and approvals

The monthly performance summary and review call explain what happened, what was changed, and what should happen next. The client remains responsible for timely access, funding, factual business claims, offer terms, required approvals, lawful source assets, lead handling, and compliance obligations that apply to the business.

## Additional-scope triggers

Separate review and approval are required for:

- another platform, business, website, country, market, language, or unrelated offer;
- additional or materially different campaign types;
- extensive landing-page, CRM, tracking, automation, or creative production;
- technical work that cannot be performed safely within the website-edit allowance;
- policy remediation or account-recovery work outside normal management;
- materially expanded reporting or meeting requirements.

## Results statement

Advertising results vary based on the market, offer, competition, budget, website, targeting, customer demand, and sales process. RielArt does not guarantee a specific number of leads, sales, revenue, rankings, or return on advertising spend.

Final payment, renewal, cancellation, suspension, and termination provisions remain subject to the signed service agreement and legally reviewed terms.
