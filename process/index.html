How it works

One clear path from first conversation to launch.

Tell RielArt about your business, confirm the right starting point, prepare the work, then launch with clear ownership and next steps.

Four clear steps

Know what is happening and what comes next.

The same organized sequence supports Brand & Website Launch, Focused Ads Management, or both services in order.

  1. 01
    Tell us about your business

    Share the goal, audience, offer, and market.

    The inquiry provides enough context to understand what your business does, what needs to improve, and where you want to reach customers.

    Useful context
    • Business and customer
    • Current website
    • Market and timeline
  2. 02
    Confirm the right starting point

    Choose the service that fits the current need.

    RielArt reviews whether the business needs a brand and website foundation, is ready for advertising, or should complete the two services in sequence.

    Review includes
    • Offer and website clarity
    • Advertising readiness
    • Google or Meta fit
  3. 03
    Build or prepare the campaign

    Complete the agreed work with organized review points.

    Brand and website work moves from direction to responsive implementation. Advertising work moves from account access and tracking to campaign setup and approval.

    Delivery includes
    • Clear scope and responsibilities
    • Consolidated feedback
    • Testing and approvals
  4. 04
    Launch, measure, and improve

    Go live with ownership and next steps documented.

    Website projects receive launch assistance and handoff. Advertising campaigns receive monitoring, data-based optimization, and a monthly performance summary.

    After launch
    • Client-owned accounts and assets
    • Tracking and verification
    • Reporting or handoff
Two services

The process adapts to the work, not the other way around.

The services are separate. A business may begin with either one, subject to advertising readiness, or use both when a stronger website should come first.

Brand & Website Launch

$599 USD one time

Brand direction, a practical visual identity, up to four core website pages, a contact form, SEO foundations, and launch assistance.

Most projects are designed to be completed in approximately three to five weeks after the required content, feedback, approvals, and account access are received.

Focused Ads Management

$349 USD per month

One Google or Meta campaign for one primary offer, with tracking, monitoring, optimization, reporting, and limited campaign-related website updates.

The service begins with a three-month initial commitment. Advertising spend is paid separately by the client directly to Google or Meta.

What keeps work moving

Clear inputs and timely decisions support a reliable launch.

Schedules and campaign launch dates depend on the agreed scope, content, access, feedback, approvals, tracking, and third-party platform readiness.

Content and access

Provide accurate business information, approved content, brand assets, and the account access required for the selected service.

Feedback and approvals

Consolidated decisions at the agreed review points help avoid delays and unplanned changes in scope.

Advertising readiness

Advertising begins after the website, tracking, campaign approvals, account access, and client-funded advertising budget are ready.
