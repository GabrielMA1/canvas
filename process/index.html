A visible delivery process

A visible path from business problem to usable handoff.

RielArt connects decisions, production, validation, and ownership through five phases. The schedule depends on scope, content readiness, feedback, integrations, and third-party dependencies; the sequence and milestones are documented before work begins.

Five phases

Know what is happening, what is needed, and what comes next.

The phases remain consistent while the detailed activities adapt to the engagement. A focused audit and a connected implementation do not require the same production plan, but both need clear decisions, validation, and handoff.

01 · Assess

Understand the goal and current state.

Review the business goal, audience, current experience, tools, constraints, source material, and relevant evidence.

Typical activities

  • Kickoff and decision-owner confirmation
  • Source-material and access checklist
  • Current-state review and constraint mapping
  • Questions, assumptions, dependencies, and risks
02 · Define

Turn findings into an actionable scope.

Set priorities, the page or workflow plan, deliverables, responsibilities, milestones, approval points, and useful measures of success.

Typical activities

  • Prioritized scope and milestone plan
  • Information architecture or workflow design
  • Feedback rhythm and approval responsibilities
  • Scope boundaries and change-control path
03 · Build

Create the focused solution in context.

Develop the agreed strategy, content structure, interface, website, automation, integration, or connected system while keeping decisions tied to the original goal.

Typical activities

  • Working drafts and contextual reviews
  • Consolidated feedback at defined checkpoints
  • Decision records and dependency tracking
  • Approved scope changes documented before added work
04 · Validate

Test the complete experience before launch.

Review responsive behavior, content, forms, accessibility, performance, metadata, analytics, integrations, error paths, and edge cases relevant to the scope.

Typical activities

  • Device and responsive QA
  • Form, link, integration, and workflow checks
  • Accessibility and performance review
  • Final content, ownership, and launch readiness
05 · Launch & Improve

Deploy, document, and prepare the next useful phase.

Launch the approved work, confirm access and ownership, provide documentation, and identify what should be monitored, maintained, or improved next.

Typical activities

  • Deployment and post-launch verification
  • Access, files, documentation, and handoff
  • Ownership and monitoring responsibilities
  • Optional ongoing support and improvement plan

Working rhythm

Clear participation keeps the work moving.

The detailed rhythm is documented for each engagement. These practices reduce avoidable delays and keep important choices with the right people.

Kickoff with the decision owners

Confirm the goal, responsibilities, source material, dependencies, communication path, and approval authority.

Consolidated feedback

Review the work at planned checkpoints and provide one coordinated response rather than disconnected revisions.

Visible change control

New requirements are assessed for impact on scope, schedule, dependencies, and cost before they are added.

Documented schedule

Timing reflects the actual scope and dependencies. Milestones are agreed before production rather than promised as a generic turnaround.

After launch

Ownership is clear, and support can continue when useful.

The handoff explains access, files, documentation, ownership, monitoring responsibilities, and recommended next steps. Optional ongoing management can cover website updates, content, analytics, advertising, performance, and automation support according to the agreed plan.
