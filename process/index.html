A clear delivery process

A clear path from business problem to launch.

Five phases keep decisions, production, testing, and handoff visible from the start.

Five phases

Know what is happening and what comes next.

The sequence stays consistent; the detail adapts to the work.

  1. 01
    Assess

    Understand the goal and current state.

    Clarify the goal, audience, current experience, constraints, and evidence.

    Typical activities
    • Kickoff and access
    • Current-state review
    • Risks and dependencies
  2. 02
    Define

    Turn findings into an actionable scope.

    Set priorities, responsibilities, milestones, and useful measures of success.

    Typical activities
    • Scope and milestone plan
    • Page or workflow design
    • Approval responsibilities
  3. 03
    Build

    Create the focused solution in context.

    Create and review the agreed strategy, website, workflow, or system.

    Typical activities
    • Working drafts
    • Contextual reviews
    • Decision tracking
  4. 04
    Validate

    Test the complete experience before launch.

    Check content, responsive behavior, accessibility, performance, forms, and integrations.

    Typical activities
    • Responsive QA
    • Form and workflow checks
    • Launch-readiness review
  5. 05
    Launch & Improve

    Deploy, document, and prepare the next phase.

    Launch the work, document ownership, and plan what should be monitored next.

    Typical activities
    • Post-launch verification
    • Files and documentation
    • Support plan
