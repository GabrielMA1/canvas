# RielArt Offer Scope

## Public offer model

RielArt has exactly two primary public services. They can be purchased separately or sequentially.

## 1. Brand & Website Launch

**Price:** $599 USD one time  
**Best for:** Businesses that need a clearer, more credible online foundation.  
**Description:** Build a clear brand and professional website your business can confidently share.  
**Primary CTA:** Start Your Launch

### Included

- Structured business and audience questionnaire
- Practical brand direction
- Positioning guidance
- Colour palette
- Typography system
- Existing-logo refinement or a simple professional wordmark
- Core messaging guidance
- Responsive business website
- Up to four core pages
- Clear calls to action
- Contact or lead form
- Basic on-page SEO
- Mobile optimization
- Basic analytics and conversion-tracking foundation
- Domain connection and launch assistance
- One organized revision round
- Client Portal access
- Final website handoff

The typical page set is Home, About, Services, and Contact. Another four-page structure may be used when it better fits the business.

### Timeline

Most Brand & Website Launch projects are designed to be completed in approximately three to five weeks after RielArt receives the required content, feedback, approvals, and account access. An exact date is confirmed only after scope and dependencies are reviewed.

### Not automatically included

- Multiple original logo concepts or a complex custom identity
- Extensive brand guidelines
- Ecommerce or membership systems
- Custom portals or software
- Advanced booking, CRM, or automation work
- Extensive copywriting from a blank page
- Photography or video production
- Unlimited revisions or pages
- Large content migrations
- Hosting or domain registration fees
- Premium software, plugins, fonts, stock assets, or other third-party costs

Requirements outside the standard scope are reviewed before work begins and require separate approval.

## 2. Focused Ads Management

**Price:** $349 USD per month  
**Commitment:** Three-month initial commitment  
**Best for:** Businesses with a suitable website and clear offer that are ready to actively promote their services.  
**Description:** Reach potential customers through a focused Google or Meta advertising campaign managed by RielArt.  
**Primary CTA:** Start Advertising

The full service definition is maintained in `RIELART-ADS-SERVICE-SCOPE.md`.

## When a business needs both

The services remain separate:

- Brand & Website Launch: $599 once
- Focused Ads Management: $349 per month
- Initial RielArt total when both begin in sequence: $948
- Following RielArt monthly payments: $349
- Advertising spend: paid separately by the client directly to Google or Meta

Public summary:

> Need both? Start with Brand & Website Launch, then add Focused Ads Management when the website is ready to attract customers.

This is an example sequence, not a third package. Buying Brand & Website Launch does not guarantee advertising results. A business with an existing suitable website may begin with Focused Ads Management after RielArt’s advertising-readiness review.

## Inquiry and payment model

All commercial calls to action lead to the project inquiry form. No public Stripe checkout or subscription link is approved for the two current offers.

Future payment links belong in `config/payment-links.json`. That internal configuration is excluded from the published site. A link may be connected to public UI only after the offer, currency, amount, payment structure, and destination have been approved and tested.

## Truth and pricing controls

- Do not describe Brand & Website Launch as cheap, budget, entry level, starter pricing, discounted, introductory, templated, new-agency, or limited-time.
- Do not create a “most popular” label.
- Do not publish a third bundle.
- Do not publish add-on prices without approval.
- Do not promise leads, sales, revenue, rankings, or advertising performance.
- Do not add public payment buttons until approved links exist.
