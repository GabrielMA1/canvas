Privacy Policy

How RielArt handles information submitted through the website and the RielArt Client Portal.

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Information we collect

RielArt collects information you voluntarily provide through the contact form, email, telephone, and other direct communications. This may include your name, email address, company, website URL, project details, and the information needed to prepare a free audit or response.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, evaluate project fit, prepare proposals, deliver agreed services, maintain records, and protect the website and business from misuse.

RielArt Client Portal

Approved clients may use the RielArt Client Portal. The portal may process a client name, approved email address, organization, project details, tasks, updates, support messages, authentication codes, session information, and basic security or activity records needed to operate the service.

Service providers

The website and portal may use Formspree for free-audit requests and contact submissions, Cloudflare for hosting and portal infrastructure, Resend for passwordless login email, and Stripe for checkout, subscriptions, and billing. These providers may process information on RielArt’s behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. RielArt does not store full payment-card details.

Cookies and local storage

The website stores your light or dark appearance preference in local storage. No advertising cookies are intentionally set by the static site itself.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for the inquiry, project, legal obligations, and business records. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask about the personal information held in relation to your inquiry, request corrections, or ask for deletion where legal and contractual requirements allow.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@rielart.com.
