View the RielArt website

Design decisions

  • The price is part of the first impression. Both services and their prices sit directly beneath the homepage headline, set like a price list.
  • Scope sits next to price. Commitments, separate advertising spend, and exclusions are shown where the price is read.
  • Inquiry before payment. Every service begins with a fit review, so the site has no checkout.
  • One ink, used flat. The deep blue of the R mark is printed as solid colour—for fields, prices, and links—without gradients or effects.
