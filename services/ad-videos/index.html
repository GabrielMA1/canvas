RielArt’s own ad videos.

These are internal RielArt projects, made in the same formats delivered to clients.

What every ad video includes.

One focused ad, built to be understood in a few seconds and placed straight into a campaign.

The video

Designed for the feed and readable with the sound off.

  • Up to 30 seconds of motion graphics
  • Animated type, your logo, photos, screenshots, and icons
  • On-screen copy written or refined for your offer
  • Royalty-free music track
  • A clear call to action at the end
  • Short clips you already own, when supplied

The files

Ready to upload to Meta, TikTok, or YouTube.

  • Vertical 9:16 for Reels, Stories, TikTok, and Shorts
  • Square 1:1 for feeds
  • MP4 files you keep
  • One organized revision round
  • Delivered through the Client Portal

Clear on what goes in.

A complete brief and assets are what make a 1–3 day turnaround possible.

What RielArt needs from you

Your brief and assets

  • Your logo, or the identity from Brand & Website Launch
  • Photos, product images, or screenshots
  • The offer and the action viewers should take
  • Brand colours and any wording that must appear
Not included

Filming and live production

  • Filming or on-location shoots
  • Actors, presenters, or UGC creators
  • Professional voiceover
  • 3D or character animation
  • Videos longer than 30 seconds
  • Commercial music beyond the royalty-free library
  • Advertising spend

Delivery and revisions

Fast, and scheduled one video at a time.

Each ad video is delivered within 1–3 business days after RielArt receives a complete brief and assets. Feedback from the organized revision round is usually returned within one business day. Videos in a Video Pack or a Growth Partner month are scheduled one after another.

Using your videos

Yours to run.

You may use delivered ad videos in your own advertising and social channels. Music comes from a royalty-free library licensed for that use, and each track’s licence terms apply. Additional revisions, longer formats, and extra versions are quoted before work begins.
