RielArt’s own ad videos.

These videos were made by RielArt for its own brand, in the same formats clients receive.

What every ad video includes.

One short ad that people understand in a few seconds, ready to post or run as an ad.

The video

Made for phones, and easy to follow with the sound off.

  • Up to 30 seconds of animation
  • Moving text, your logo, photos, screenshots, and icons
  • On-screen text written for your offer
  • Music included
  • A clear message at the end, like “Book now” or “Call today”
  • Short clips you already have can be added

The files

Ready to upload to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube.

  • Vertical version for Reels, Stories, TikTok, and Shorts
  • Square version for regular posts
  • Video files you keep
  • One round of changes
  • Delivered through the Client Portal

What we need from you.

Send these and your video can be ready in 1–3 business days.

From you

A few simple things

  • Your logo
  • Photos of your work, products, or place
  • What you’re offering and what viewers should do (call, book, visit)
  • Your brand colours and any words that must appear
Not included

Filming and live production

  • Filming or on-location shoots
  • Actors, presenters, or UGC creators
  • Professional voiceover
  • 3D or cartoon character animation
  • Videos longer than 30 seconds
  • Paid music beyond the included library
  • Your ad budget

Timing and changes

Fast, one video at a time.

Each video is ready within 1–3 business days after RielArt has everything listed above. Your round of changes is usually done within one business day. If you order several videos, they’re made one after another.

Using your videos

Yours to use.

You can use your videos in your own ads and on your social media. The music comes from a library licensed for that use. Extra changes, longer videos, and extra versions are quoted before work begins.
