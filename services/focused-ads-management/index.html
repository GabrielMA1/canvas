The standard scope

One focused campaign with clear boundaries.

The $349 monthly service includes one advertising platform—Google Ads or Meta Ads—selected after RielArt reviews the business.

Campaign foundation

Review readiness, choose the appropriate platform, and configure the initial campaign.

  • One business and one website
  • One country or clearly defined regional market
  • One advertising language
  • One primary service or offer
  • Campaign strategy and setup
  • Advertising copy and campaign creative
  • Basic conversion tracking
  • Client Portal access

Ongoing management

Monitor useful signals, make data-based adjustments, and explain the work.

  • Regular campaign monitoring
  • Data-based optimization
  • Monthly performance summary
  • One monthly review call of up to 30 minutes
  • Up to one hour of advertising-related website or landing-page changes
  • Campaign-message and tracking review
  • Access and approval coordination

Advertising spend is not included. The client pays the platform budget directly to Google or Meta and keeps control of the payment method.

Platform recommendation

RielArt recommends Google or Meta based on how customers discover and choose your service.

The review considers search behaviour, market, budget, website readiness, conversion path, visual assets, competition, and average customer value.

Google Search Ads

Reach active searchers.

Best when potential customers are already searching for the service.

  • Home, professional, local, urgent, or B2B services
  • High-intent customer searches
  • Offers with a clear search need

These are potential fits, not automatic qualification.

Meta Ads

Build discovery and response.

Best when the offer benefits from strong visuals, awareness, promotions, or reaching customers before they actively search.

  • Beauty, fitness, events, or consumer services
  • Visual transformations and promotions
  • New or unfamiliar offers

The final platform is confirmed after the business review.

Platform-specific scope

What the first campaign can include.

Google Search Ads

Campaign structure and responsive messaging—not a fixed count of repeated ads.

  • One Google Ads account and one Search campaign
  • Up to three focused ad groups
  • One responsive search ad per ad group
  • Multiple unique headlines and descriptions
  • Keyword, match-type, and search-term review
  • Negative keyword management
  • Geographic, schedule, and device review
  • Relevant sitelinks, callouts, and call assets
  • Website-form conversion tracking where technically possible
  • Phone-call conversion tracking where technically possible
  • Regular monitoring, optimization, and reporting

Google responsive search ads combine and test multiple headlines and descriptions. RielArt manages the campaign structure, targeting, messaging, tracking, and optimization rather than selling a fixed number of repeated advertisements.

Meta Ads

Campaign creative using approved client media, licensed stock assets, website content, brand graphics, motion typography, or other suitable materials.

  • One Meta advertising account
  • Facebook and Instagram placements through Meta Ads Manager
  • One Leads or Sales campaign
  • Up to two ad sets when justified
  • Meta campaigns normally begin with approximately three to five ad variations built around a focused set of creative concepts
  • Launch variations may combine static designs, copy variations, carousels, or short-form video where suitable
  • Ongoing management includes up to two refreshed creative variations per month when useful
  • One of those two variations may be a short-form video ad
  • Basic audience, geography, and placement configuration
  • Meta Pixel setup and event testing where supported
  • Conversions API review where supported by the platform and scope

Included short-form videos are typically focused 10–15 second edits created for the campaign. On-location filming, actors, UGC creator sourcing, professional voice talent, advanced animation, complex production, and larger video requirements are quoted separately.

The two-variation allowance applies only to Meta campaigns, and creative is refreshed based on campaign needs rather than produced unnecessarily. A video counts as one of the two included variations; it is not an additional third creative. Google Search campaigns do not include monthly video production.

Website readiness

Advertising works better when the destination is ready.

Already have a website? RielArt will review it before advertising begins.

What RielArt reviews

The goal is to identify issues that would make traffic difficult to understand, trust, or convert.

  • Clear offer, location, and service information
  • Mobile usability and page speed
  • Working contact method and form
  • Clear call to action and basic trust signals
  • Privacy information and tracking capability
  • Advertising-policy compatibility
  • Message consistency
  • Conversion path

When changes are needed

A new website is not automatically required.

When important issues would limit campaign performance, RielArt may recommend correcting the website or completing Brand & Website Launch first. Purchasing the $599 service does not guarantee advertising results.

Campaign-related website changes

Up to one hour per month, used where it supports the active campaign.

Unused website-update time does not roll over.

Possible within the allowance

Minor, campaign-linked changes

Adjust a headline or CTA, change a button, replace a small image, update a short section or promotion, correct contact details, improve a simple form, correct tracking, or make a small mobile-layout correction.

Separate scope

New pages and substantial development

New pages, full-page redesigns, new websites, large layout changes, ecommerce, booking systems, advanced integrations, extensive copywriting, unrelated technical work, and unlimited changes are not included.

When RielArt did not build the website, edits depend on a review of the platform, access, code, plugins, theme, and technical condition. Work that cannot be completed safely within the allowance requires separate approval.

Ownership, budget, and limits

The client keeps the account and controls the platform spend.

Account ownership

No personal password sharing.

The client owns the advertising account, payment method, campaign history, and data. RielArt receives the manager, partner, or task access required to provide active management. Access can be removed after termination and handoff.

Advertising budget

Generally begins around $500 USD per month.

The appropriate budget depends on the industry, location, competition, offer, platform, and goals. $500 is a starting reference, not a guaranteed sufficient budget or a promise of leads. Competitive industries may require more.

International scope

One country or one defined regional market is included.

The standard service also includes one advertising language and one primary offer. Multi-country or multilingual campaigns require separate review because they may need different targeting, copy, budgets, landing pages, policies, currencies, time zones, and reporting.

Three-month initial commitment

Time to configure, learn, and improve.

The initial period allows time to configure tracking, build and launch the campaign, gather useful data, and make informed improvements. It does not guarantee meaningful results within three months.

Results

No performance guarantee.

Advertising results vary based on the market, offer, competition, budget, website, targeting, customer demand, and sales process. RielArt does not guarantee a specific number of leads, sales, revenue, rankings, or return on advertising spend.
