Google or Facebook & Instagram? RielArt recommends the right one for you.

It depends on how your customers look for businesses like yours. You don’t need to decide before getting in touch. Curious? Read Google Ads vs Facebook Ads.

Google Search Ads

Show up when people search.

Best when people already search Google for what you offer—like “plumber near me” or “wedding photographer in Toronto.”

  • Home, local, professional, or urgent services
  • Customers who are ready to call or book
  • Things people already know they need

These are common fits, not automatic approval.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Get noticed while people scroll.

Best when your work looks great in photos or video, you have a promotion, or people don’t know about your offer yet.

  • Before-and-afters, promotions, and openings
  • New offers people aren’t searching for yet

A closer look at each platform.

For the curious: the details of what RielArt sets up.

Google Search Ads

Your ad appears in Google search results for the right searches.

  • One Google Ads account and one search campaign
  • Up to three groups of ads, each focused on one type of search
  • Several headlines and descriptions that Google mixes and tests
  • Choosing the searches you show up for—and the ones you don’t
  • Where and when your ads show
  • Extra links, your phone number, and short highlights in your ad
  • Tracking of website forms and phone calls where possible
  • Regular checks, improvements, and reporting

Google responsive search ads combine and test multiple headlines and descriptions to find what works best. So instead of selling a set number of ads, RielArt manages who sees your ads, what they say, and how well they work.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Campaign creative using approved client media, licensed stock assets, website content, brand graphics, motion typography, or other suitable materials.

  • One Facebook & Instagram advertising account (Meta Ads Manager)
  • Your ads shown on Facebook and Instagram
  • One campaign focused on inquiries or sales
  • Up to two audiences when useful
  • Facebook & Instagram campaigns usually start with three to five ads using your photos or videos
  • Up to two fresh image ads a month when useful
  • New animated video ads are available as Ad Videos, or two are included every month with Growth Partner
  • Choosing the area and the people who see your ads
  • The Meta Pixel (a small tracking code) set up and tested where possible

Ads Management includes simple image ads. New animated video ads are a separate service—Ad Videos for $99 USD each—or two are included every month with Growth Partner. Filming, actors or UGC creators, and professional voiceover are not included in either.

Fresh image ads are made only when they’ll help, not just to fill a quota. Google search ads are text ads, so they don’t include video.

Ads work better when your website is ready.

Already have a website? RielArt will check it before your ads start.

What RielArt checks

Anything that would stop visitors from trusting you or getting in touch.

  • Your offer, location, and services are clear
  • The site works well and loads quickly on phones
  • The contact form and phone number work
  • It’s obvious what visitors should do next
  • Privacy information and tracking
  • Nothing breaks Google’s or Facebook’s ad rules
  • The ad and the website say the same thing
  • Getting in touch takes as few steps as possible

If changes are needed

You don’t automatically need a new website.

If something important would hold your ads back, RielArt may suggest fixing it first, or starting with Brand & Website Launch ($349). A new website does not guarantee ad results.

You keep your ad account and control your budget.

Your account

No password sharing.

The client owns the advertising account, payment method, results history, and data. RielArt gets the access needed to manage your ads, and you can remove it at any time.

Your ad budget

Most businesses start around $500 USD a month.

The right budget depends on your industry, area, competition, and goals. $500 is a common starting point, not a promise that it’s enough or that it brings a set number of leads. Busy industries may need more.

Where you advertise

One country or one clearly defined region.

The standard service also covers one language and one main offer. Ads in several countries or languages are reviewed separately, because they need different targeting, text, budgets, and reporting.

No long contract

The service continues month to month.

There’s no minimum term: the service continues month to month until you cancel. Ads usually need a few weeks to set up, gather data, and improve, so most businesses give them at least two to three months. New clients who commit to 3 months get 10% off their first month.

Results

No guaranteed numbers.

Results depend on your market, offer, competition, budget, website, and how you follow up with customers. RielArt does not guarantee a specific number of leads, sales, revenue, rankings, or return on ad spend.
