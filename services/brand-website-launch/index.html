Everything you need to look professional online.

A focused project with a clear list of what’s included—so you know exactly what you’ll get.

Your brand

Make your business easy to understand and recognise.

  • A short questionnaire about your business and customers
  • Advice on how to describe what you do
  • Colours and fonts that fit your business
  • A touch-up of your logo, or a simple new one made from your name
  • Clear buttons that tell visitors what to do next
  • One round of changes
  • Access to the Client Portal

Your website

A site customers can trust and use easily.

  • Works on phones, tablets, and computers
  • Up to four pages—usually Home, About, Services, and Contact
  • A contact form so customers can reach you
  • Basic setup so Google can find your site
  • Visitor tracking so you can see what’s working
  • Connected to your domain (your web address) and put live
  • All files and logins handed over to you
  • Need more pages? Extra pages are $79 each

Clear about what’s included.

Anything outside this list is quoted before work begins, so there are no surprises.

Included

One focused business website

The four pages can be different ones if that suits your business better. Each page needs a clear purpose and the text and photos you approve.

Not included

Bigger projects are quoted separately

  • Several logo options or a full brand book
  • Online shops or member areas
  • Custom customer portals
  • Advanced booking systems or automation
  • Photography or filming
  • Moving a large existing website
  • Unlimited changes or pages

Payment

Two simple payments.

The $399 USD price is paid in two parts: $200 when the project starts and $199 when your site goes live. Payment details are confirmed in your agreement.

Other costs

The $399 covers RielArt’s work.

Hosting, your domain name, paid software or plugins, fonts, and stock photos are paid separately. RielArt tells you about any known costs before you agree.

Timing

Fast when we have what we need.

Most projects take about three to five weeks once RielArt has your text, photos, feedback, and account access. An exact date is confirmed once the scope is agreed.

Extra

Business Email & Workspace Setup

Covers one existing business domain and up to 3 users. Not included in the $399 Brand & Website Launch, but included with Growth Partner. Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 subscription costs are separate, and the client owns the account.
