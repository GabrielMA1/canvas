What is included

The essentials for a credible business presence.

The service focuses on the foundation most growing service businesses need—not an open-ended creative or software engagement.

Direction and identity

Make the business easier to understand and recognize.

  • Structured business and audience questionnaire
  • Practical brand direction and positioning guidance
  • Core messaging guidance
  • Colour palette and typography system
  • Existing-logo refinement or a simple professional wordmark
  • Clear calls to action
  • One organized revision round
  • Client Portal access

Website and launch

Turn the direction into a useful, shareable customer experience.

  • Responsive business website
  • Up to four core pages
  • Contact or lead form
  • Basic on-page SEO
  • Mobile optimization
  • Basic analytics and conversion-tracking foundation
  • Domain connection and launch assistance
  • Final website handoff
  • Home, About, Services, and Contact are the typical starting structure
Scope boundaries

Focused enough to stay clear.

Requirements outside the standard service are reviewed before work begins and need separate approval.

Included foundation

One focused business website

The exact four-page structure can change when another structure better fits the business. Every page still needs a clear purpose and approved source content.

Not automatically included

Complex identity, software, or production

Multiple logo concepts, extensive guidelines, ecommerce, memberships, custom portals, advanced booking or CRM work, extensive automation, photography, video, large migrations, and unlimited revisions or pages are outside the standard scope.

Third-party costs

The $599 fee covers RielArt’s approved work.

Hosting, domain registration, premium software, plugins, fonts, paid stock assets, and other third-party costs are not included. RielArt identifies known required costs before approval where reasonably possible.

Content and timing

Progress depends on complete inputs and timely decisions.

Most projects are designed to be completed in approximately three to five weeks after RielArt receives the required content, feedback, approvals, and account access. An exact date is not guaranteed before scope and dependencies are confirmed.

From inquiry to launch

A short, organized project sequence.

  1. 01

    Share the business context

  2. 02

  3. 03

  4. 04
