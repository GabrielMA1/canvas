Delivery standards

Professional delivery is part of the work.

The solution matters, but so do the decisions, safeguards, ownership, and information that make it usable after delivery.

Clear scope and ownership

Goals, deliverables, responsibilities, dependencies, and approval points are documented.

Responsive and accessible foundations

Content, interfaces, interactions, and forms are considered across devices and different access needs.

Performance and search readiness

Structure, media, metadata, internal links, and measurement are planned from the beginning.

Measurable paths

Important customer actions are defined so the business can evaluate and improve the journey.

Maintainable handoff

Access, files, documentation, ownership, and documented next steps are treated as deliverables.

Human judgment around AI

Repeatable work can be automated while sensitive decisions retain a clear route to a person.
